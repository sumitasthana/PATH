# Plasticity After Task-unlearning in Hypernetworks (PATH)

Wiki for the study. It asks whether unlearning a task restores the model's
ability to learn new ones, as the UnCLe paper claims. The repository was
earlier called CARK; the URLs still use that name and the old links still work.

- [Experiment trajectory](https://github.com/sumitasthana/CARK/wiki/Experiment-trajectory): latest findings and experiment records by date.
- [Concepts and processes](https://github.com/sumitasthana/CARK/wiki/Concepts-and-processes): what the model does and how to read its results.
- [Model diagnostics](https://github.com/sumitasthana/CARK/wiki/Model-diagnostics): inspect weights, gradients, and Fisher scores.
- [Anchor paper](https://github.com/sumitasthana/CARK/wiki/An-Unlearning-Framework-for-Continual-Learning): the paper we are reproducing.
